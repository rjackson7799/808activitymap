About Ono Seafood

A Kapahulu institution since 1996, Ono Seafood is a no-frills takeout counter that many locals will tell you serves the best poke on Oahu. Fish is cut fresh each morning; the spicy ahi and shoyu styles are the ones regulars line up for. There's no dining room — just a couple of stools — so most people grab a bowl and eat at the beach a few minutes away.

It's cash only, closes when the fish runs out, and the line moves faster than it looks. Come hungry, come early, and don't overthink it — the combo bowl is the move.
